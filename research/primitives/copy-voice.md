# Copy Voice — Ranked Rules

Lifted and condensed from `research/sources/copywriting-system.md` (full 40-rule ranked rulebook) and the Dentologic brand wiki copywriting voice. When in doubt, read the full sources.

## Top-priority rules (memorise)

1. Use **oralstack**, not Dentologic.
2. Lead with the dental job: book, chart, bill, image, message, recall, discharge.
3. One idea per sentence.
4. Active voice. Concrete nouns. Strong verbs.
5. Sentence case for headings, buttons, and labels.
6. CTAs name the action ("Book a demo", "See the workflows" — never "Get started", "Learn more", "Submit", "Continue").
7. Labels stand alone — would the label still make sense out of context?
8. Errors must state cause + fix.

## Banned phrases (audit fails on these)

`all-in-one`, `seamless`, `seamlessly`, `supercharge`, `unlock`, `transform`, `best-in-class`, `cutting-edge`, `next-generation`, `revolutionary`, `delight`, `effortless`, `effortlessly`, `streamline` (when used as decoration rather than a real verb).

## Banned claim patterns (audit fails — must be qualified or held)

- "Guaranteed [revenue / growth / savings]"
- "Automated diagnosis"
- "Zero risk", "100% [anything compliance-related]"
- "HIPAA-certified", "SOC 2 certified" — until we are certified.
- Round-number ROI without per-customer attribution: "30% more patients" with no name attached fails the rule.

## Required dental-specific anchors

Every page of website copy must contain at least one of: *patient, chart, chair, front desk, recall, discharge, imaging, case notes, treatment, billing, hygiene, dentist, hygienist*.

## Templates (apply by surface)

| Surface | Template | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Hero headline | Verb-stack of jobs + brand promise | "Book, chart, bill, image, message. The operating system for modern dental clinics." |
| CTA primary | `[Verb] [object]` | "Book a demo" |
| CTA secondary | `[Verb] [alternative]` | "See the workflows" |
| Field label | `[Object or data type]` | "Patient name", "Billing email" |
| Error | `[Problem]. [Fix].` | "Couldn't reach the schedule. Refresh the page." |
| Success | `[Outcome].` | "Appointment booked." |
